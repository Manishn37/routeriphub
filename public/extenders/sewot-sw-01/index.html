SEWOT SW-01 WiFi Extender — 192.168.188.1 Login & Setup Guide

If you searched 192.168.188.1 expecting to open your SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender, you are in the right place—but the page will only load when your phone or computer is connected directly to the extender. 192.168.188.1 is the default management IP address used by the SEWOT SW-01 WiFi repeater and similar Wireless-N extender models.

Quick Answer

192.168.188.1 is the default admin IP address for the SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender.

Connect your phone or computer directly to the extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port, then enter http://192.168.188.1 in your browser’s address bar.

Default Login Credentials:
  • Username: admin
  • Password: admin (or leave blank / password on some units)

Some units may use a blank password, password, or credentials printed on the extender label. If the extender was configured before, use the admin password you created during setup.

Open http://192.168.188.1 → All Wi-Fi Extenders

SEWOT SW-01 Login Details

Setting Default Value
Default IP address 192.168.188.1
Setup URL http://192.168.188.1
Default username admin
Default password admin
Default Wi-Fi network Often Wireless-N, WiFi-Repeater, SEWOT, or a name printed on the label
Reset method Hold Reset/RST button for about 8–10 seconds

The default IP is mainly a fallback address. After the SEWOT SW-01 connects to your router in Repeater or Access Point mode, your router may assign it a new dynamic LAN IP through DHCP.

Network Role: Extender vs. Router

Device Typical Address What It Controls
Main router / gateway Often 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 Internet connection, DHCP, firewall, primary Wi-Fi
SEWOT SW-01 before setup 192.168.188.1 Extender setup, Wi-Fi extension, local settings
SEWOT SW-01 after pairing New DHCP-assigned IP Extended Wi-Fi coverage and connected devices
Phone, laptop, or smart TV Assigned by router Ordinary client device on your network

So, if 192.168.188.1 does not open your main router’s admin page, that is expected. This address is intended to reach the SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender, not the router itself.

Why 192.168.188.1 May Not Load

192.168.188.1 is a private local IP address, not a public website. It works only when your device is connected directly to the SEWOT SW-01 extender.

If you are connected to your main router’s Wi-Fi instead of the extender, the browser cannot reach 192.168.188.1. Connect to the extender’s setup Wi-Fi network or use an Ethernet cable, then try again.

Step-by-Step: Set Up the SEWOT SW-01

Step 1

Plug in the extender

Place the SEWOT SW-01 near your main router during the first-time setup. Wait for it to power on completely before connecting to it.

Step 2

Connect to the extender

On your phone or computer, open the Wi-Fi list and connect to the SEWOT SW-01’s default Wi-Fi network. The network name may look like Wireless-N, WiFi-Repeater, SEWOT, or another name printed on the device label. If the extender has an Ethernet port, you can also connect a computer directly using an Ethernet cable.

Step 3

Open the setup page

In your browser address bar, enter:
• http://192.168.188.1
Do not type the address into a Google or Bing search box. Enter it directly into the browser address bar.

Step 4

Log in

For the SEWOT SW-01, use:
• Username: admin
• Password: admin
If that does not work, try a blank password, password, or the credentials printed on the extender label. If the extender was configured before, use the admin password created during original setup.

Step 5

Choose your home Wi-Fi network

The setup wizard will scan for nearby Wi-Fi networks. Select your main router’s network, enter its Wi-Fi password, and follow the prompts to complete the extender setup.

Step 6

Move the extender to its final location

After setup, unplug the extender and place it halfway between your router and the area with weak Wi-Fi. Do not place it too far from the router, because a weak incoming signal will also create a weak extended signal.

If 192.168.188.1 Will Not Load

1. Connect directly to the extender

Disconnect from your main router’s Wi-Fi and connect to the SEWOT SW-01’s Wi-Fi network. Alternatively, connect a computer to the extender using an Ethernet cable and disable Wi-Fi on that computer.

2. Turn off mobile data and VPN

On a phone, disable mobile data, VPNs, and proxy settings. The extender’s setup network may not have internet access, and your phone may try to route the request through cellular data instead.

3. Use the browser address bar

Type http://192.168.188.1 directly into the browser address bar. Do not search for it in Google, Bing, or another search engine.

4. Check your device IP address (Set Temporary Static IP)

If your device shows an IP address starting with 169.x.x.x or 0.0.0.0, the extender may not have assigned a valid DHCP address. Set a temporary static IP in the 192.168.188.x range, such as 192.168.188.100, with subnet mask 255.255.255.0 and gateway 192.168.188.1. After setup, switch your device back to automatic DHCP.

5. Try another browser or private window

Open an Incognito or InPrivate window, disable browser extensions, and try Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. This helps rule out cached DNS results, extensions, or browser-security warnings.

6. Find the extender’s new IP

If the extender is already paired, log into your main router and open the Attached Devices, Connected Devices, or DHCP Client List page. Find the SEWOT SW-01, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that IP into your browser.

Factory Reset the SEWOT SW-01

Reset the extender only if you cannot log in, are moving it to a new router, or the setup page will not load after troubleshooting.

  1. Keep the SEWOT SW-01 powered on.
  2. Find the small Reset or RST button or pinhole on the side, bottom, or back panel.
  3. Press and hold it with a paperclip for about 8–10 seconds.
  4. Release when the indicator lights blink or the extender restarts.
  5. Wait for the extender to reboot completely.
  6. Reconnect to the extender’s Wi-Fi network and open http://192.168.188.1.

Reset times can vary by model. If 8–10 seconds does not work, try holding the button for up to 15 seconds while watching the LED behavior.

SEWOT SW-01 vs. Router Login

Login Type Address Default Credentials
SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender 192.168.188.1 Often admin / admin; some units use a blank password
Main router Usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 Router-specific credentials or password created during setup

Do not confuse the SEWOT SW-01 login with the main router login. The SEWOT SW-01 extends your existing Wi-Fi signal; it does not replace your router or manage your internet connection.

Common Mistakes

  • Trying 192.168.188.1 while connected to the main router. Connect directly to the SEWOT SW-01’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port first.
  • Assuming 192.168.188.1 opens the main router. It is the SEWOT SW-01’s factory management address, not a typical router gateway address.
  • Using the wrong credentials. Many SEWOT SW-01 units use admin / admin, but some use a blank password, password, or label-printed credentials.
  • Using mobile data during setup. Turn off mobile data, VPNs, and proxies before opening the local setup page.
  • Resetting before checking the DHCP list. If the extender is already paired, its new IP may be listed in your router’s client list.

Common Typos to Watch Out For

Misspelling the IP address is the most common reason the setup portal fails to load:

192.168.l88.1 → http://192.168.188.1
192.168.188.l → http://192.168.188.1
192.168.188.1.com → http://192.168.188.1
192.168.188.1 login → http://192.168.188.1
192.168.18.1 → http://192.168.188.1

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 192.168.188.1 a real website?

No. It is a private local IP address used by the SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender. It only works when your phone or computer is connected directly to the extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port.

What is the default password for the SEWOT SW-01?

Many SEWOT SW-01 units use admin as both the username and password. Some models use a blank password, password, or credentials printed on the device label.

Why does 192.168.188.1 not work?

The most common reason is that you are connected to your main router instead of the SEWOT SW-01. Other causes include mobile data, VPN interference, browser search behavior, or the extender already receiving a new DHCP-assigned IP address.

How do I find my SEWOT SW-01 after setup?

Log into your main router and open the connected-devices or DHCP client list. Find the SEWOT SW-01, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that address in your browser.

Can I use 192.168.188.1 on a phone?

Yes. Connect your phone to the SEWOT SW-01’s Wi-Fi network, turn off mobile data and VPN, then enter http://192.168.188.1 in the browser address bar.

🎯 The Bottom Line

192.168.188.1 almost always means you are trying to reach the SEWOT SW-01 WiFi extender, not your main router. Connect directly to the extender, enter http://192.168.188.1 in the browser address bar, and use the correct credentials for your model. If the page will not load, check the extender’s new DHCP-assigned IP in your router’s client list.
