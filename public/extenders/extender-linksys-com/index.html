extender.linksys.com — Linksys Extender Setup & 192.168.1.1 Login

If you searched extender.linksys.com expecting to open your Linksys extender, you are in the right place—but the page may not load unless you are connected directly to the extender. extender.linksys.com is Linksys’s local setup address for its RE-series range extenders, while 192.168.1.1 is the fallback IP address used when the extender cannot detect a DHCP server.

⚡ Quick Answer

extender.linksys.com is the local setup portal for Linksys Wi-Fi range extenders, and 192.168.1.1 is the default fallback IP address when no DHCP server is detected.

To access either one, connect your phone or computer directly to the extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port first.

Default Login Credentials:
  • Username: Leave blank
  • Password: admin

If you are setting up a new extender, connect to the default network named Linksys Extender Setup - xxx, where xxx is the last three characters of the extender’s MAC address. Then open http://extender.linksys.com, http://192.168.1.1, or http://RE7350-xxx in your browser.

Open http://extender.linksys.com → Open http://192.168.1.1 All Wi-Fi Extenders

Which Linksys Extenders Use This Address?

extender.linksys.com and the 192.168.1.1 fallback IP apply to Linksys’s Max-Stream and RE-series range extenders.

Linksys Extender Family Example Models Default Fallback IP Setup URL
Max-Stream AC series RE6350, RE6500, RE6700, RE7000 192.168.1.1 (when no DHCP detected) http://extender.linksys.com
Max-Stream / Wi-Fi 6 AX series RE7310, RE7350 192.168.1.1 (when no DHCP detected) http://extender.linksys.com or http://RE7350-xxx

The RE7350-xxx address uses the last three characters of the extender’s MAC address. It is useful when the standard setup URL does not resolve on your device.

Network Role: Extender vs. Router

Device Typical Address What It Controls
Main Linksys router or another router Often 192.168.1.1 Internet connection, DHCP server, firewall, primary Wi-Fi
Linksys extender before setup 192.168.1.1 fallback address Extender setup wizard, Wi-Fi extension, local settings
Linksys extender after pairing New DHCP-assigned IP Extended Wi-Fi coverage and connected devices
Phone, laptop, or smart TV Assigned by router Ordinary client device on your network

This is why 192.168.1.1 can be confusing: it may open either your main router or your Linksys extender, depending on which network you are connected to and whether the extender is already configured.

Why extender.linksys.com May Not Load

extender.linksys.com is a local management address, not a normal public website. It works because the Linksys extender intercepts the local DNS request when your device is connected directly to it.

If you are connected to your main router’s Wi-Fi instead of the extender, the browser cannot resolve extender.linksys.com. In that case, connect to the extender’s setup SSID or use an Ethernet cable, then try again.

Step-by-Step: Set Up a Linksys Extender

Step 1

Plug in the extender

Place the Linksys extender near your main router during initial setup. Wait for it to power on fully before connecting to it.

Step 2

Connect to the extender

On your phone or computer, open the Wi-Fi list and connect to Linksys Extender Setup - xxx (replace xxx with the last three characters of the extender’s MAC address). This default setup network is open, so it does not require a password.

Step 3

Open the setup page

In your browser address bar, enter one of the following:
• http://extender.linksys.com
• http://192.168.1.1
• http://RE7350-xxx — for compatible RE7350 models

Step 4

Log in

For a Linksys RE-series extender, leave the Username field blank and enter:
• Password: admin
If the login was changed previously, use the password created during the original setup.

Step 5

Choose your home Wi-Fi network

The setup wizard will scan for available Wi-Fi networks. Select your main router’s network, enter its Wi-Fi password, and follow the prompts to complete the extension setup.

Step 6

Move the extender to its final location

After setup, unplug the extender and place it halfway between your router and the Wi-Fi dead zone. Avoid placing it too far from the router, because a weak incoming signal will also create a weak extended signal.

If extender.linksys.com Will Not Load

1. Connect directly to the extender

Disconnect from your main router’s Wi-Fi and connect to Linksys Extender Setup - xxx. Alternatively, connect a computer to the extender using an Ethernet cable and disable Wi-Fi on that computer.

2. Turn off mobile data and VPN

On a phone, turn off mobile data, VPNs, and proxy settings. The extender’s setup network may not have internet access, and your phone may try to route the request through cellular data instead.

3. Use the browser address bar

Type http://extender.linksys.com directly into the address bar. Do not search for it in Google, Bing, or another search engine.

4. Try the fallback IP

Enter http://192.168.1.1 in the browser. This fallback address works when the Linksys extender is in factory condition and cannot detect a DHCP server.

5. Try the model-specific URL

For a Linksys RE7350, try http://RE7350-xxx, replacing xxx with the last three characters of the extender’s MAC address.

6. Check for a security warning

If your browser shows a privacy, certificate, or “Your connection is not private” warning, choose Advanced and then Proceed to extender.linksys.com (unsafe). This is common with local extender pages that use self-signed certificates.

7. Find the extender’s new IP

If the extender is already paired, log into your main router and open the Attached Devices, Connected Devices, or DHCP Client List page. Find the Linksys extender, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that IP into your browser.

Factory Reset a Linksys Extender

Reset the extender only if you cannot log in, are moving it to a new router, or the setup page will not load after troubleshooting.

  1. Keep the Linksys extender powered on.
  2. Find the physical Reset button on the side or bottom panel.
  3. Press and hold it for about 10 seconds on most models, including the RE7350.
  4. For the RE6500, hold it for about 5 seconds until the LEDs flash.
  5. Release the button and wait for the extender to reboot.
  6. Reconnect to Linksys Extender Setup - xxx and open http://extender.linksys.com.

You can also reset some Linksys extenders through the web interface by going to Administration > Factory Defaults > Restore Factory Defaults.

Linksys Router vs. Extender Login

Login Type Address Default Credentials
Linksys RE-series extender http://extender.linksys.com or 192.168.1.1 fallback Blank username, password admin
Linksys router or mesh node http://myrouter.local or Linksys app Router admin password or Recovery Key-based recovery

Do not confuse the extender login with the Linksys router login. Linksys mesh routers and nodes use a different recovery process and may require the product-label Recovery Key if the admin password is forgotten.

Common Mistakes

  • Trying extender.linksys.com while connected to the main router. Connect directly to the extender’s SSID or Ethernet port first.
  • Assuming 192.168.1.1 always opens the extender. It can also open your main router, depending on the network you are connected to.
  • Entering a username when none is required. For Linksys RE-series extenders, leave the username field blank and enter admin as the password.
  • Using the wrong setup SSID. The correct network is Linksys Extender Setup - xxx, using the last three MAC-address characters.
  • Resetting before checking the DHCP list. If the extender is already paired, its new IP may be listed in your router’s client list.

Common Typos to Watch Out For

Misspelling the Linksys extender domain or IP address is the most common reason the setup portal fails to load:

extenderlinksys.com → http://extender.linksys.com
extender.linksys.com login → http://extender.linksys.com
192.168.1.l → http://192.168.1.1
192.168.1.1.com → http://192.168.1.1

Frequently Asked Questions

Is extender.linksys.com a real website?

No. It is a local setup address used by Linksys range extenders. It only works when your phone or computer is connected directly to the extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port.

What is the default password for a Linksys extender?

For Linksys RE-series extenders, leave the username blank and use admin as the default password. This applies only if the extender has not been configured before.

Why does 192.168.1.1 open my router instead of my extender?

Because 192.168.1.1 is also a common router gateway address. If you are connected to your main router’s Wi-Fi, the browser will usually open the router instead of the extender.

How do I find my Linksys extender after setup?

Log into your main router and open the connected-devices or DHCP client list. Find the Linksys extender, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that address in your browser.

Can I use the Linksys app instead of extender.linksys.com?

For Linksys routers and mesh systems, the Linksys app is commonly used. For standalone RE-series extenders, the local setup URL and web interface are the primary methods described in the extender documentation.

🎯 The Bottom Line

extender.linksys.com almost always means you are trying to reach a Linksys RE-series Wi-Fi extender, not your main router. Connect directly to the extender, enter the URL in the browser address bar, and use a blank username with admin as the password; if it will not load, try the 192.168.1.1 fallback IP or check the router’s DHCP client list for the extender’s new address.
