ap.setup — Wavlink Extender Setup & 192.168.10.1 Login

If you searched ap.setup expecting to open your Wavlink extender, you are in the right place—but the page will only load when your phone or computer is connected directly to the Wavlink device. ap.setup is Wavlink’s local setup address for its Wi-Fi repeaters and outdoor access points, while 192.168.10.1 is the default management IP address.

⚡ Quick Answer

ap.setup is the local setup portal for Wavlink Wi-Fi repeaters and outdoor access points, and 192.168.10.1 is the default IP address used to access the device’s admin page.

Connect your phone or computer directly to the Wavlink extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port, then enter one of these addresses in your browser:

Access Method Address When to Use It
Wavlink setup domain http://ap.setup First choice for most Wavlink repeaters & access points
Default fallback IP http://192.168.10.1 Use when the domain does not load or no DHCP server is detected
Alternate setup URLs http://wavlogin.link / http://waplogin.link Supported on select Wavlink repeater revisions
Default Login Credentials:
  • Username: admin (or leave blank)
  • Password: admin

If the Wavlink device was configured before, use the admin password you created during setup. If you cannot remember it, a factory reset is required.

Open http://ap.setup → Open http://192.168.10.1 All Wi-Fi Extenders

Which Wavlink Devices Use ap.setup?

ap.setup is commonly used by Wavlink wireless repeaters, range extenders, and outdoor access points. Some models may also use wavlogin.link or waplogin.link, but ap.setup remains one of the most common local setup addresses.

Wavlink Device Type Example Models / Series Default IP Setup URL
Wireless repeaters WL-WN527A2, WAVLINK-N, WAVLINK-AC 192.168.10.1 http://ap.setup
Outdoor access points Wavlink outdoor AP and repeater models 192.168.10.1 http://ap.setup
Multi-segment models Some Wavlink repeater variants 192.168.20.1, 192.168.30.1, or 192.168.40.1 http://ap.setup

The default IP is mainly a fallback address. After the Wavlink device connects to your router in Repeater or Access Point mode, your router may assign it a new dynamic LAN IP through DHCP.

Network Role: Extender vs. Router

Device Typical Address What It Controls
Main router / gateway Often 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 Internet connection, DHCP, firewall, primary Wi-Fi
Wavlink extender before setup 192.168.10.1 Extender setup, Wi-Fi extension, local settings
Wavlink extender after pairing New DHCP-assigned IP Extended Wi-Fi coverage and connected devices
Phone, laptop, or smart TV Assigned by router Ordinary client device on your network

So, if 192.168.10.1 does not open your main router’s admin page, that is expected. This address is intended to reach the Wavlink extender or access point, not the router itself.

Why ap.setup May Not Load

ap.setup is not a public website. It is a local address that works only when your device is connected directly to the Wavlink extender or access point.

If you are connected to your main router’s Wi-Fi instead of the Wavlink device, the browser cannot resolve ap.setup. Connect to the extender’s setup Wi-Fi network or use an Ethernet cable, then try again.

Step-by-Step: Set Up a Wavlink Extender

Step 1

Plug in the extender

Place the Wavlink extender near your main router during the first-time setup. Wait for it to power on completely before connecting to it.

Step 2

Connect to the extender

On your phone or computer, open the Wi-Fi list and connect to the Wavlink extender’s default Wi-Fi network. The network name may look like WAVLINK, Wavlink-Ext, WAVLINK-N, or another name printed on the device label. If the extender has an Ethernet port, you can also connect directly using an Ethernet cable.

Step 3

Open the setup page

In your browser address bar, enter one of the following:
• http://ap.setup
• http://192.168.10.1
• http://wavlogin.link
• http://waplogin.link
Do not type the address into a Google or Bing search box. Enter it directly into the browser address bar.

Step 4

Log in

For many Wavlink repeaters and access points, use:
• Username: admin or leave blank
• Password: admin
If that does not work, check the label on the extender or use the password created during the original setup.

Step 5

Choose your home Wi-Fi network

The setup wizard will scan for nearby Wi-Fi networks. Select your main router’s network, enter its Wi-Fi password, and follow the prompts to complete the extender setup.

Step 6

Move the extender to its final location

After setup, unplug the extender and place it halfway between your router and the area with weak Wi-Fi. Do not place it too far from the router, because a weak incoming signal will also create a weak extended signal.

If ap.setup Will Not Load

1. Connect directly to the extender

Disconnect from your main router’s Wi-Fi and connect to the Wavlink extender’s Wi-Fi network. Alternatively, connect a computer to the extender using an Ethernet cable and disable Wi-Fi on that computer.

2. Turn off mobile data and VPN

On a phone, disable mobile data, VPNs, and proxy settings. The extender’s setup network may not have internet access, and your phone may try to route the request through cellular data instead.

3. Use the browser address bar

Type http://ap.setup directly into the browser address bar. Do not search for it in Google, Bing, or another search engine.

4. Try the fallback IP

Enter http://192.168.10.1 in the browser. This address works when the Wavlink extender is in factory condition or cannot detect a DHCP server.

5. Try the alternate Wavlink URLs

If ap.setup does not load, try:
• http://wavlogin.link
• http://waplogin.link
These local management domains work only while you are connected to the Wavlink extender’s network or Ethernet port.

6. Check your device IP address (Set Temporary Static IP)

If your device shows an IP address starting with 169.x.x.x or 0.0.0.0, the extender may not have assigned a valid DHCP address. Set a temporary static IP in the 192.168.10.x range, such as 192.168.10.100, with subnet mask 255.255.255.0 and gateway 192.168.10.1. After setup, switch your device back to automatic DHCP.

7. Try another browser or private window

Open an Incognito or InPrivate window, disable browser extensions, and try Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari. This helps rule out cached DNS results, extensions, or browser-security warnings.

8. Find the extender’s new IP

If the extender is already paired, log into your main router and open the Attached Devices, Connected Devices, or DHCP Client List page. Find the Wavlink extender, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that IP into your browser.

Factory Reset a Wavlink Extender

Reset the extender only if you cannot log in, are moving it to a new router, or the setup page will not load after troubleshooting.

  1. Keep the Wavlink extender powered on.
  2. Find the small Reset or RST button or pinhole on the side, bottom, or back panel.
  3. Press and hold it with a paperclip for about 3 seconds.
  4. Release when all indicator lights turn on momentarily or begin flashing.
  5. Wait for the extender to reboot completely.
  6. Reconnect to the extender’s Wi-Fi network and open http://ap.setup or http://192.168.10.1.

Some Wavlink support guides recommend holding the Reset button for 6–8 seconds or up to 15 seconds, depending on the model. If 3 seconds does not work, try holding it longer while watching the LED behavior.

Wavlink Extender vs. Router Login

Login Type Address Default Credentials
Wavlink repeater or access point http://ap.setup or 192.168.10.1 Often admin / admin; some models use a blank username
Main router Usually 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 Router-specific credentials or password created during setup

Do not confuse the Wavlink extender login with the main router login. A Wavlink extender extends your existing Wi-Fi signal; it does not replace your router or manage your internet connection.

Common Mistakes

  • Trying ap.setup while connected to the main router. Connect directly to the Wavlink extender’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port first.
  • Assuming 192.168.10.1 opens the main router. It is the Wavlink extender’s factory management address, not a typical router gateway address.
  • Using the wrong credentials. Many Wavlink devices use admin / admin, but some models use a blank username or different default password.
  • Using mobile data during setup. Turn off mobile data, VPNs, and proxies before opening the local setup page.
  • Resetting before checking the DHCP list. If the extender is already paired, its new IP may be listed in your router’s client list.

Common Typos to Watch Out For

Misspelling the Wavlink setup domain or IP address is the most common reason the portal fails to load:

apsetup → http://ap.setup
ap.setup.com → http://ap.setup
ap.setup login → http://ap.setup
192.168.l0.1 → http://192.168.10.1
192.168.10.l → http://192.168.10.1
192.168.10.1.com → http://192.168.10.1

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ap.setup a real website?

No. It is a local setup address used by Wavlink Wi-Fi repeaters and outdoor access points. It only works when your phone or computer is connected directly to the Wavlink device’s Wi-Fi network or Ethernet port.

What is the default password for a Wavlink extender?

Many Wavlink repeaters and access points use admin as both the username and password. Some models use a blank username, while others may use a different default password printed on the device label.

Why does ap.setup not work?

The most common reason is that you are connected to your main router instead of the Wavlink extender. Other causes include mobile data, VPN interference, browser search behavior, or the extender already receiving a new DHCP-assigned IP address.

How do I find my Wavlink extender after setup?

Log into your main router and open the connected-devices or DHCP client list. Find the Wavlink extender, copy its assigned IP address, and enter that address in your browser.

Can I use ap.setup on a phone?

Yes. Connect your phone to the Wavlink extender’s Wi-Fi network, turn off mobile data and VPN, then enter http://ap.setup or http://192.168.10.1 in the browser address bar.

🎯 The Bottom Line

ap.setup almost always means you are trying to reach a Wavlink Wi-Fi repeater or outdoor access point, not your main router. Connect directly to the Wavlink device, enter http://ap.setup or http://192.168.10.1 in the browser address bar, and use the correct credentials for your model. If the page will not load, check the extender’s new DHCP-assigned IP in your router’s client list.
