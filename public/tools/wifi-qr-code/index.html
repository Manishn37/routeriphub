Wi-Fi QR Code Generator & Printable Card

Create encrypted WPA2/WPA3 QR codes for seamless smartphone guest network logins. Download high-resolution PNG or printable sign.

How to Generate a Printable Wi-Fi QR Code

Sharing Wi-Fi passwords with guests, customers, and family members manually often leads to typos, frustration, and security risks. Using this free, client-side tool, you can generate an instant connection QR code in seconds:

  1. Enter Network Name (SSID): Type your wireless network name exactly as it appears in your router configuration (SSIDs are case-sensitive).
  2. Input Wi-Fi Password: Provide your network password. All rendering takes place locally in your browser memory via the HTML5 Canvas API—your credentials are never transmitted over the internet.
  3. Select Encryption Standard: Most modern routers use WPA/WPA2/WPA3. For open hotspot networks, select None.
  4. Toggle Hidden Network (Optional): If your router has "SSID Broadcast" disabled, check the "Hidden Network" box so mobile devices know to query the hidden beacon.
  5. Download or Print: Click "Download PNG" for digital sharing, or "Print Sign" to print an elegant guest display card for your office, Airbnb, coffee shop, or home.

How Wi-Fi QR Codes Work (Standard Schema Protocol)

Mobile operating systems (iOS 11+ and Android 10+) feature built-in support for the standard Wi-Fi configuration string format. When encoded as a 2D matrix barcode, the standardized payload follows the syntax:

WIFI:T:WPA;S:YourNetworkSSID;P:YourSecretPassword;H:false;;

Here is a breakdown of the protocol parameters:

  • T: Authentication Type (WPA for WPA/WPA2/WPA3, WEP for legacy WEP, or nopass for open).
  • S: Service Set Identifier (SSID / Network Name). Special characters are properly escaped.
  • P: Pre-Shared Key (Wireless Password).
  • H: Hidden SSID flag (true or false).

How to Scan and Connect on iOS and Android

🍎 Apple iOS (iPhone & iPad)

  1. Open the native Camera app from your home screen or Control Center.
  2. Frame the Wi-Fi QR code within the camera viewfinder.
  3. A yellow banner will appear reading: "Join [Network Name] Network".
  4. Tap the banner, then tap "Join" to authenticate immediately.

🤖 Google Android

  1. Open your Camera app or swipe down to Quick Settings and tap Internet.
  2. Tap the QR scanner icon next to "Add network" or use Google Lens.
  3. Point your camera at the QR code.
  4. Tap the popup prompt: "Connect to Wi-Fi".

Security Advantages of Sharing Wi-Fi via QR Code

Generating a Wi-Fi QR code offers multiple critical security advantages over writing passwords on a whiteboard or sticky note:

  • Enables High-Entropy Passwords: Because users do not have to type characters manually on small phone keyboards, you can use a strong 24-character random password that is immune to brute-force cracking.
  • Encourages Guest Network Isolation: Create a dedicated QR code for your router's Guest Wi-Fi SSID. Visitors can access the internet without having access to your home NAS, internal servers, smart cameras, or router administration portal.
  • Eliminates Shoulder Surfing: Guests scan directly without you having to vocalize or display plaintext passwords across crowded rooms.
