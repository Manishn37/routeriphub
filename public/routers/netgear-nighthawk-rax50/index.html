Nighthawk AX6 Setup: Complete Installation and Configuration Guide

The fastest Nighthawk AX6 setup uses the Nighthawk app. Plug the router into your modem, power it on, join its Wi-Fi network from your phone, and follow the app prompts. You can also finish setup in a browser at routerlogin.net. Either path takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

What is the Nighthawk AX6?

The Nighthawk AX6 is Netgear's 6-stream AX5400 Wi-Fi 6 router. The main model numbers are RAX50, RAX50v2 and RAX54Sv2. The 5GHz band runs 4x4 and reaches up to 4.8Gbps, and the 2.4GHz band runs 2x2 at up to 600Mbps. Those are theoretical link rates, so real speeds will be lower.

The 5GHz radio supports 160MHz channels, and the router works with older Wi-Fi devices.

Important Naming Warning: Netgear also sells a cable modem router called the Nighthawk AX6 (CAX30), which is an AX2700 unit. This guide covers the AX5400 router. If your box says CAX30, the steps are close, but the modem needs activation with your cable provider.

What you need before you start

Gather four things before you unbox the router. It saves you from stopping halfway.

  • An active internet plan and a working modem. Cable, fiber and DSL modems all work.
  • A smartphone with the Nighthawk app installed, or a laptop with a browser.
  • Your ISP account details. DSL lines often need a PPPoE username and password.
  • A spot for the router. Put it in a central, open location off the floor, away from microwaves and thick walls.

Check the box contents as well. You should find the router, a power adapter, an Ethernet cable and a quick start guide.

Step-by-step installation

Follow these steps in order. Do not skip the modem restart, because it makes the modem hand its connection to the new router.

  1. Power off your modem. If it has a backup battery, remove it for a few seconds.
  2. Connect the modem's LAN port to the router's yellow Internet (WAN) port with the Ethernet cable.
  3. Power the modem back on and wait two minutes for its lights to settle.
  4. Plug in the router and press the Power button.
  5. Wait for the power LED to turn solid white. That takes a couple of minutes.
  6. Connect your phone or laptop to the default Wi-Fi network. The name and password are printed on the router's label.

If you replace an older router, copy its Wi-Fi name and password into the new setup. Your phones, TVs and smart speakers will then reconnect without any changes.

Finish setup with the Nighthawk app or a browser

You have two ways to complete the configuration. The app is the easier path for most people.

Option 1: Nighthawk App

Download it from nighthawk-app.com or your phone's app store, and keep your phone connected to the router's Wi-Fi. Open the app, sign in with a Netgear account, and follow the prompts. The app also handles firmware updates and speed tests later.

Option 2: Web Browser

Open http://www.routerlogin.net on a device connected to the router. The addresses routerlogin.com and http://192.168.1.1 also work. The username is admin, and the password is the one you set during first-run setup.

Both fields are case-sensitive. Some older Netgear guides list password as the default password. If setup never asked you to create one, try it, then change it at once. Store the new password in a password manager.

Configure Wi-Fi settings

Good Wi-Fi settings matter more than the raw speed rating. Spend five minutes here.

  • Network name. Choose a unique SSID. Avoid your name or address.
  • Security. Use WPA3 if all your devices support it. Otherwise pick WPA2/WPA3 mixed mode.
  • Password. Use 14 or more characters.
  • Smart Connect. Leave it on if you want the router to steer devices between bands. Turn it off if an older smart-home gadget will not join the network.
  • Guest network. Enable it for visitors and IoT devices so they stay off your main network.

The 2.4GHz band reaches farther and passes through walls better. The 5GHz band is much faster at short range. Put laptops, consoles and streaming boxes on 5GHz when they sit in the same room as the router.

For the best throughput, wire your gaming PC or TV with Ethernet. A cable bypasses interference entirely.

Update the firmware

Update the firmware right after setup. Netgear ships fixes for bugs and security flaws through new releases, and an out-of-date router is an easy target.

To update from a browser:

  1. Log in at routerlogin.net.
  2. Go to Advanced, then Administration, then Router Update.
  3. Click Check.
  4. Click Yes when the router offers new firmware.

Menu names can shift slightly between firmware versions. Do not close the browser or unplug the router during the update. Interrupting it can corrupt the firmware. The router restarts on its own when it finishes.

You can also upload a file manually. Download the firmware for your exact model from Netgear's support page, then upload it through the same Router Update screen.

Fix common setup problems

Most issues have simple causes. Try these before you contact support.

  • routerlogin.net won't open. Type http://192.168.1.1 instead. Turn off any VPN and mobile data, and clear the browser cache.
  • No internet after setup. Restart the modem, then the router. Confirm the cable sits in the yellow WAN port.
  • Slow Wi-Fi. Move the router to a central spot and switch your device to the 5GHz band.
  • Forgot the admin password. Use the factory reset below.
  • Old devices won't connect. Turn off Smart Connect and use WPA2.

If your ISP modem is a combo unit with its own Wi-Fi, you may see double NAT. Put the ISP device into bridge mode, or ask your provider to do it.

Factory reset the AX6

A factory reset erases every custom setting, including your Wi-Fi name, password and port rules. Use it only when you are locked out or when other fixes fail.

  1. Leave the router powered on.
  2. Find the Reset button on the back panel.
  3. Press and hold it with a paper clip until the power LED blinks. That takes roughly 7 to 10 seconds.
  4. Release it and wait for the router to restart.
  5. Run the setup process again from step 6 above.

Write down your ISP login details first, since you will need to enter them again.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the default login for the Nighthawk AX6?

The username is admin. The password is the one you create during setup. Some older guides list password as a default.

Is the Nighthawk AX6 the same as the RAX50?

Yes. RAX50 is the main model number, and RAX50v2 and RAX54Sv2 are related versions.

Does the AX6 work as a modem?

No. The AX5400 router needs a separate modem. Only the CAX30 variant has a built-in cable modem.

Does it support Wi-Fi 6E?

No. It supports Wi-Fi 6 on 2.4GHz and 5GHz only.
