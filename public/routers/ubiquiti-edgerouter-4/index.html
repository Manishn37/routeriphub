How to Factory Reset Ubiquiti EdgeRouter 4 (ER-4)

Hardware Reset Instructions:

With device powered ON, use a pin to hold the recessed Reset button for 10 seconds until the LED ring or LCD screen announces 'Restoring to Factory Defaults'. Release and wait 3 minutes.
