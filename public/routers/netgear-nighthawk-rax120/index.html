Netgear Nighthawk AX12 Setup Guide

Netgear Nighthawk AX12 (RAX120) Setup

Netgear Nighthawk RAX120 setup takes about 15 minutes. Connect the modem to the yellow Internet port, power on the router, then finish setup in the Nighthawk app or at routerlogin.net. If your internet plan is faster than 1Gbps, you must also switch the WAN to the Multi-Gig port or enable port aggregation.

Author: Vikas Behl • Model: Netgear Nighthawk AX12 (RAX120 / RAX120v2) • Standard: Wi-Fi 6 (AX6000 12-Stream) • Updated: November 2026

What is the Nighthawk AX12 (RAX120)?

The RAX120 is a 12-stream AX6000 Wi-Fi 6 router. Netgear sells it as the Nighthawk AX12. It has eight antennas and 8-stream MU-MIMO, which lets four 2x2 devices stream at the same time.

The port layout is the main reason to buy it:

Port Quantity Speed
Gigabit Ethernet (1 WAN, 4 LAN) 5 10/100/1000Mbps
Multi-Gig Ethernet 1 5G/2.5G/1G, configurable as LAN or WAN
USB 3.0 2 For storage and printers

The Multi-Gig port reaches 5Gbps. Dual Gigabit port aggregation can give a wired internet connection of up to 2Gbps. A single Gigabit WAN port tops out at 1Gbps, so the extra ports matter only if your plan is faster.

What you need before you start

Prepare these items so setup runs without interruptions.

  • A modem or fiber ONT with an active internet plan.
  • A smartphone with the Nighthawk app, or a computer with a browser.
  • ISP login details if your line uses PPPoE.
  • The Ethernet cable from the box. For plans above 1Gbps, you need a cable rated for the speed, such as Cat6 or better.
  • A central spot with open air around the router. Attach the antennas before powering on.

Check your modem's ports. If your plan exceeds 1Gbps, the modem needs a high-speed Ethernet port, such as 2.5G, to use the Multi-Gig connection.

Install the router

Follow these steps in order.

  1. Connect the Internet port on the router to the modem or incoming Ethernet line.
  2. Attach the antennas, connect the power adapter, and plug it in. Press the power button if the router has one.
  3. Find the default Wi-Fi name (SSID) and password on the label. The QR code on the label works with the app.
  4. Wait for the router to finish starting. The Internet LED shows connection status.
  5. Join the default Wi-Fi from your phone or computer.
Modem Power Cycle Tip: Restart the modem if the router does not detect the internet. Turn off both devices, reboot the modem first, then reconnect the router and try again.

Complete setup

There are two ways to finish. The app is faster, and the browser works when the app fails.

Nighthawk app. Install it, scan the QR code on the label, and follow the prompts. The app sets your Wi-Fi name, password and login for you.

Browser. Connect to the router by Wi-Fi or a LAN cable and open http://www.routerlogin.net. If an app prompt appears, scroll down and choose the option for people without a compatible smartphone. Then follow the installation assistant through Internet detection and setup.

On later logins, the username is admin. The password is the one you created the first time you logged in. Both are case-sensitive. If routerlogin.net will not load, type 192.168.1.1.

Set up Multi-Gig internet

Skip this section if your plan is 1Gbps or less. For faster plans, you have two options, and the router needs a manual change for either one.

Multi-Gig WAN port

  1. Connect the Multi-Gig port to the high-speed Ethernet port on the modem.
  2. Log in at routerlogin.net.
  3. Open the Advanced settings and set the Multi-Gig port to act as the Internet (WAN) port.
  4. Save, then restart the modem and router.

This path needs a modem that supports speeds over 1Gbps and an ISP plan to match.

Port aggregation

This combines the Internet port with LAN port 1 for up to 2Gbps. The modem must also support link aggregation. The setup lives in the router's Advanced menu. Port 1 stops working as a normal LAN port after you enable it.

Choose the Multi-Gig WAN if your modem has a 2.5G or 5G port. Pick aggregation only when the modem offers two Gigabit ports and your plan sits between 1 and 2Gbps. Most US cable and fiber plans at 2Gbps and above favor the Multi-Gig route.

Configure Wi-Fi

Spend five minutes on these settings after the connection works.

  • Name and password. Pick a unique SSID and a password of 14 or more characters.
  • Security. Use WPA3 where devices support it, or WPA2/WPA3 mixed mode.
  • Smart Connect. Leave it on to let the router assign bands. Turn it off if a smart-home device fails to join.
  • Guest network. Enable it for visitors and IoT devices.
  • Wired devices. Plug in consoles, desktops and TVs by cable. Use the Multi-Gig LAN port for a NAS or a high-end PC if you set the WAN elsewhere.

The router has no 6GHz band. It is a dual-band Wi-Fi 6 model, so it will not reach Wi-Fi 6E speeds.

Update the firmware

Update the firmware right after setup. Netgear's manual process takes about two minutes.

  1. Connect your computer to the router with an Ethernet cable.
  2. Download the latest RAX120 firmware from Netgear Support and unzip it if needed.
  3. Log in at routerlogin.net.
  4. Go to Advanced, then Administration, then Firmware Update or Router Update.
  5. Choose the file ending in .img or .chk and click Upload.

Do not go online, switch off the router or touch its settings until it finishes restarting. Menu names change a little between firmware versions.

Some long-time RAX120 owners on community forums suggest a factory reset after each firmware update. They say it clears old low-level settings. That is anecdotal advice, so use it only when a new release causes odd behavior.

Factory reset the RAX120

A reset erases every custom setting. Use it if you are locked out or the router misbehaves after an update.

There are two methods. The first is software: log in at 192.168.1.1, open Advanced, go to Administration, then Backup Settings, and click Erase. The second is the Reset button on the back panel. Hold it with a paper clip until the power LED blinks.

Important: Write down your ISP login details first. You will need to repeat setup afterward.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the default login for the RAX120?
The username is admin. The password is what you set during first-time setup.
How fast is the RAX120?
It carries an AX6000 rating. Real speeds depend on distance, walls and your devices.
Does the RAX120 support Multi-Gig?
Yes. It has one 5G/2.5G/1G port that you can set up as LAN or WAN.
Can I set it up without the app?
Yes. Use routerlogin.net in a browser.
Does it support Wi-Fi 6E?
No. It is a dual-band Wi-Fi 6 router.
